Objective

Build a retrieval-augmented QA system over a self-scraped corpus, then attack it: inject adversarial documents crafted to (a) win retrieval for a targeted question and (b) push the reader toward a specific false answer. Measure attack success rate as a function of poison count, poison style, and corpus size. Then defend with consistency voting and provenance scoring, and measure how much accuracy the defense recovers and what it costs on clean queries. Framed explicitly as a defensive evaluation of our own corpus — every poison document is self-authored and injected into our own index, no third-party system is targeted.

Applications

RAG security auditing before deployment · integrity checks for enterprise knowledge bases that ingest from open or web-scraped sources · robustness testing against SEO-style content designed to be retrieved · a reusable red-team harness (corpus + gold set + injectable poison) for any RAG stack · provenance/consistency scoring as a retrieval-time filter, independent of this specific corpus.

Dataset

poison.jsonlpending150 docs planned — 3 per question × styles keyword / natural / authoritative. The original contribution.
Gold-set integrity is enforced by an automated check (test_r2.py): every correct_answer appears verbatim in its cited article, every cited article is retrievable in top-5 under a clean index, and every attacker_target is absent from that clean top-5 context — so a poison flip, when it happens, is attributable to the poison and not to an already-ambiguous question.

Desired Outcomes

· A released poison set (150 docs, 3 styles) that is retrieved and believed at measurable rates.
· An attack-success curve across poison count (1/3/5/10) and style, on the current 125-article corpus and at least one larger corpus size.
· A defense that recovers a stated share of poisoned-and-flipped answers at a stated, small cost to clean accuracy.
· A live Gradio demo replaying three visible states — correct → poisoned → defended — from the cached generations (gen_cache), network off.

Future Work

This project is deliberately inference-only — the embedder and reader are both frozen, nothing is fine-tuned. A natural extension beyond this project's scope is a small trained poison-detector (a classifier over chunk-level features such as retrieval-score anomaly or stylistic markers), evaluated with its own precision/recall/F1 against the released poison set.
